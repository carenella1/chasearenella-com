About this field guide

Chase Arenella writes about AI-augmented leadership, systems thinking, and decision-making under pressure, drawing on agile delivery and competitive gaming.

What this site is

This is a reference shelf. Every page covers one concept or answers one question, in plain language, with enough depth to use it the same day. New guides are added every week.

The guides lean on three sources: agile delivery, where teams learn by shipping in short cycles; systems thinking, which looks at how structure drives behavior; and competitive gaming, which turns decision-making under pressure into something you can practice and review.

How it differs from the essays

The main site holds longer essays that argue a point. This site is the glossary behind them: shorter, more definitional, and built to be skimmed.
