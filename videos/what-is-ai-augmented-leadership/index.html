The full written guide: What Is AI-Augmented Leadership?

Transcript

AI-augmented leadership means using AI as an extension of your attention, so you spend more time on judgment, people, and direction, while the decisions and accountability stay with you. Automation replaces a task. Augmentation expands a person. An augmented leader uses AI to read fifty reports, surface the three that matter, and then thinks hard about those three.

That matters because leadership is mostly judgment under uncertainty. AI can improve the inputs. It can't own the call. In a normal week, the gains are unglamorous.

Meeting notes become decisions, owners, and dates within minutes. A Monday summary shows what changed since last week. You edit AI drafts and run quick scenario checks. Getting real value takes three skills.

Framing precise questions. Verifying which outputs to trust. And designing repeatable routines instead of one-off prompts. These are the same skills good leaders already use with people.

Avoid using AI to produce more; use it to decide better. And don't hide its role. Tell your team where AI was used and who checked it. More from Chase Arenella at chase arenella dot com.
