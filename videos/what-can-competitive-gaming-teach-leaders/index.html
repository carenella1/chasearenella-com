What Can Competitive Gaming Teach Leaders?

A video by Chase Arenella · November 25, 2026 · 67 seconds

Learn how competitive gaming builds leadership skills like fast decisions, honest review, and clear callouts under pressure. You'll also see where the comparison between games and work breaks down.

The full written guide: What Can Competitive Gaming Teach Leaders?

Transcript

Competitive gaming trains the same skills leadership needs, it just compresses the feedback into minutes. Most workplaces give slow, blurry feedback. You make a call and learn months later whether it worked. In a game, you see the result in seconds.

A player can make hundreds of decisions in an evening and review them the next day. Few leaders get that many repetitions in a year. Strong leads share the same habits. They commit to a read with incomplete information and adjust as new facts arrive.

They review their own replays without ego. They spot shifts in the dominant strategy. And they manage tilt, resetting after a loss instead of spiraling. Good callouts are brief: what, where, and what to do about it.

The same discipline improves meetings and incident response. Say the fact, the location, and the ask, in that order. Agree on roles early, so everyone knows who makes the final call. Still, work has no clear rules, equal starts, or definite end.

Take the decision habits, not the idea that colleagues are opponents. More from Chase Arenella at chase arenella dot com.
