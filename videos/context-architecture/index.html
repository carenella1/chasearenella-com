Context Architecture: Designing Where Team Knowledge Lives

A video by Chase Arenella · November 25, 2026 · 68 seconds

Learn what context architecture is and why scattered information quietly taxes every team decision. See the three questions that fix it, why rules beat new tools, and how it makes AI assistants useful.

The full written guide: Context Architecture

Transcript

Context architecture is the deliberate design of where your team's knowledge lives, who can find it, and how it moves between tools. Most teams never choose their information layout. It accumulates. Decisions sit in chat, plans in docs, status in a tracker, and the reasons behind it all in someone's head.

Together, these tools impose a hidden tax. Every decision starts with a search. Across a team and a year, that adds up to weeks of lost time, and decisions made on partial information. The fix is to answer three questions and write the answers down.

Where does each kind of information live? What is the source of truth when two tools disagree? And how does context travel between homes? A new tool without rules becomes one more place to search.

Rules travel, and they survive any tool change. AI assistants are only as good as the context they can reach. Fragmented knowledge makes them confident and wrong. So start small.

Pick the information people search for most, give it one home, and redirect every question there for a month. More from Chase Arenella at chase arenella dot com.
