The full written guide: What Is Psychological Safety, and Why Do Fast-Moving Teams Need It More?

Transcript

Psychological safety is the shared belief that you can raise concerns, ask questions, and admit mistakes without being punished or embarrassed. It is not comfort. It does not mean low standards or constant agreement. A safe team can be demanding.

The difference is that bad news travels quickly to the people who can act on it. Fast-moving teams need more of it, because speed shortens the time to catch errors. A doubt kept private for one day can become a week of wrong work. In gaming, calling out a misplay right away lets the team adjust in the same round.

Hiding it costs the next three. Low safety rarely looks like conflict. It looks like quiet. Meetings end in agreement, then objections appear in private chats.

To build it this week, go first by naming a mistake you made. Thank people who flag problems, before asking whose fault it was. Review the system, not the person. And ask, what would make this plan fail?
