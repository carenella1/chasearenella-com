How Do You Ask AI the Right Questions as a Leader?

A video by Chase Arenella · November 25, 2026 · 66 seconds

Learn how leaders can get sharper answers from AI by framing questions around a real decision with context, constraints, and a clear format. See how to ask for friction, run a pre-mortem, and keep ownership of the call.

The full written guide: How Do You Ask AI the Right Questions as a Leader?

Transcript

Ask AI about a specific decision, not a topic, because generic questions get generic answers. The tool returns the most likely response to what you type. Ask how to improve team performance, and you get the average answer. That advice is true for everyone, so it helps no one.

The fix is not clever wording. It is specificity. Start with the choice you face. Should we cut the roadmap from six projects to three?

That beats asking how to prioritize. Describe your situation. Name the options, the constraints that cannot move, and where you lean and why. Then say what format you need, like a one-page memo.

Next, ask for friction, not agreement. AI tends to work inside your framing. Ask for the strongest case against your lean. Try a pre-mortem.

Assume this plan failed in six months. What are the three most likely causes? Treat the first answer as a draft, and work in narrow rounds. Leave out sensitive data, and check any claim that would change what you do.

The tool supplies options. You own the decision. More from Chase Arenella at chase arenella dot com.
