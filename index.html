A field guide to decision-making, systems thinking, and AI-augmented leadership.

Each guide explains one idea in a few minutes: what it is, why it matters, and how to use it on a real team. The ideas come from agile delivery, systems thinking, and competitive gaming, where decisions are fast and feedback is honest.

Recently added

  • Which Decisions Should Stay Human When You Use AI?AI can draft, sort, and recommend, but some decisions should stay with people. A simple test for deciding which calls to keep, and why.
  • What Is Systems Thinking for Teams?Systems thinking means looking at how a team's structure produces its results. A practical introduction with examples a team lead can use this week.
  • What Is AI-Augmented Leadership?AI-augmented leadership means using AI to widen what a leader can see and do, while keeping judgment and accountability human. What it looks like day to day.
  • What Can Competitive Gaming Teach Leaders?Competitive gaming trains fast decisions, honest review, and team communication under pressure. Five skills that transfer directly to leading teams.

Concepts

  • Constraints as StrategyChoosing constraints on purpose (fewer projects, tighter scope, fixed time) often produces better results than keeping every option open. How to use them.
  • Context ArchitectureContext architecture is deciding where a team's knowledge lives and how it flows. Why it matters more than which tools you pick, and how to start.
  • Decision LoopsA decision loop is the cycle of noticing, deciding, acting, and learning. How to map your team's loop and speed it up without making it sloppy.
  • Feedback LatencyFeedback latency is the delay between doing something and learning whether it worked. Why it quietly ruins team performance and how to shorten it.
  • Meta AwarenessThe meta is the strategy most players treat as best. Meta awareness is noticing when it starts to shift, in games, markets, and teams.
  • Signal vs. NoiseSignal is information that should change a decision; noise is everything else. How to tell them apart, and where AI filtering helps or backfires.
  • Tilt ManagementTilt is the spiral of bad decisions that follows frustration. What competitive players do to catch it early, and how leaders can use the same habits.
